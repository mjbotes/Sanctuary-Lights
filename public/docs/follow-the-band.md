# Follow the band by sound

No backing tracks, no click, no MIDI clock? The lights can **listen to the band**:

- **The beat** drives effects, pads and busking (Pulse, Chase, Move…), so they stay in time with the drummer.
- **How big the music is** sets the brightness: a quiet verse is softer, the full chorus is as you programmed it.

It works on top of everything else: your looks, songs and service parts don't change.

## What you need

- **Best:** a feed from the sound desk into the lighting computer's audio input (an aux send, or a USB audio interface). It hears exactly the band, not the room.
- **Also fine:** a microphone in the room, or the computer's own microphone if it's near the stage.

## Switch it on

1. Setup → **Follow the band by sound**.
2. Choose the input, then tap **🎤 Listen on this computer**. Allow the microphone if asked.
3. Watch the **beat dot** flash and the **BPM** settle within a few seconds of the band playing. The meter shows how loud it is.

The computer that listens remembers it and starts listening again when the app opens. Other screens show *Listening on …*.

## Getting it right

- **Twice too fast or too slow?** Bands are often felt in double or half time. Tap **×2** or **÷2**; it's remembered.
- **The beat sets the tempo:** on by default. It steps aside when **MIDI clock** comes in or the app's **tracks are playing**, because those know the exact tempo. The tempo display then says *by ear* when it comes from the band.
- **Brightness follows the music:**
  - **How much** sets how dim the quietest moments get (e.g. 50% brightness).
  - **Which lights:** all of them, or only some groups. A common choice is the back wash and the movers, with the front light on faces left steady.
  - It only works in parts that follow the songs, like **Worship**. During the **sermon**, **hosting** or a **video**, it does nothing ([Service parts](service-parts.md)).
- The loudness is compared with the loudest moment of the last few minutes, so a quiet song isn't dimmed all the way through. Silence counts as quiet, and between songs no beats are sent.

## Turn it off

Tap **■ Stop listening**. If no news comes from the listening computer for 3 seconds (it was closed, or the cable came out), everything goes back to normal by itself.

## Good to know

- It needs no internet; all the listening happens on the computer.
- Strong reverb or a very busy mix can make the beat wander. A desk feed with mostly kick and snare works best.
- See also [Live control, pads and effects](live-effects.md) for the effects the beat drives.
