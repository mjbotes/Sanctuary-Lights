# Sanctuary Lights guide

Sanctuary Lights runs your church's lights. It can follow your backing tracks and ProPresenter by itself, or a volunteer can run it with a few big buttons. Pick the guide that fits what you need to do.

## I'm running lights this Sunday

- [Volunteer quick card](volunteer-card.md): the one page to read if you're helping out. Print it and keep it at the desk.
- [Running a service](sunday.md): what to check before the service, what to do during it, and how to finish up.
- [When something goes wrong](troubleshooting.md): no lights, the wrong lights, nothing from the tracks, stuck dark after a video…

## I'm setting it up

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): install, the setup wizard, and the test page.
2. [Connecting lights and other gear](connections.md): USB-DMX, Art-Net, sACN, MIDI, OSC / Stream Deck, iPads.
3. [Looks, cues and scenes](looks-and-cues.md): add your lights, make looks, and build a cue list.
4. [Songs](songs.md): plan lights per song section, driven by Playback, Ableton or a volunteer.
5. [Service parts](service-parts.md): worship, hosting, sermon, videos, baptism…
6. [Service order, Planning Center and schedule](service-order.md): import Sunday's plan, step through the service, and set lights that change by themselves.
7. [ProPresenter](propresenter.md): follow slides, dim for videos, and match screen colours.

## Going further

- [Live control, pads and effects](live-effects.md): busking, strobes, chases, moving-head effects, presets and selection tricks.
- [Tracks](tracks.md): play backing tracks in the app, change the key and tempo, click and guide cues, and live auto-tune.
- [3D stage](stage-3d.md): your church in 3D, MVR import, and screens and LED walls.
- [Safety, PINs and backups](safety.md): who can change what, undo, Panic, snapshots and updates.
- [Glossary](glossary.md): what all the words mean.

> **Tip:** the **?** button at the bottom of every screen opens the guide for the screen you're on.
