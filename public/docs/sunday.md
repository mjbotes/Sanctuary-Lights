# Running a service

This guide is for the person responsible for lights on a Sunday. It assumes the show is already set up. If it isn't, start with [Getting started](getting-started.md).

## The day before (or at rehearsal)

- **Check the set list.** On the **Songs** tab, put the songs in the order they will be played, and make sure every song has its sections (Intro, Verse, Chorus…). See [Songs](songs.md).
- **New song?** Add it, and give its sections looks. Section names like "Chorus 2" pick the "Chorus" look by themselves.
- **Tracks:** if the band uses **Playback** or **Ableton**, check each new song sends a MIDI note at each section (see [Songs → MIDI](songs.md)). If you play the tracks in this app, press **Prepare set** on the **Tracks** tab so key and tempo changes are ready.
- **ProPresenter:** song presentations should have the **same name** as the songs here. Slide groups called Verse, Chorus, Bridge… then move the lights. See [ProPresenter](propresenter.md).
- **Special moments** (baptism, communion, dedication): add a [service part](service-parts.md) for each, with its own look.

## Before people arrive (15 minutes)

1. **Switch on** the lights, the DMX interface or node, and the lighting computer. Open Sanctuary Lights.
2. Go to **Setup → Test** and check the dots:
   - **DMX out** should be green.
   - **MIDI in:** ask the tracks operator to play a few seconds of a song; it should show what arrived.
   - **ProPresenter** should say *Connected*.
   - **Audio** (if the app plays the tracks): play a test tone on each output.
3. Tap **Blink every light in turn** and watch the stage. Each light should blink white when its name is shown. A light that doesn't blink isn't getting DMX: check its cable, address and power.
4. Tap **Pre-service** (if you have that part), or show your walk-in look.

## During the service

Usually you only switch the **service parts**, and the rest happens by itself:

| Moment | What you do | What happens by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Walk-in / countdown | Tap **Pre-service** | A countdown video dims the lights while it plays |
| Worship | Tap **Worship** (or let ProPresenter pick it) | Tracks or slides change the lights per section |
| Welcome and announcements | Tap **Hosting** | Songs and screen colours stop changing the lights |
| Videos | Nothing | The lights dim to the Video part, and come back when it ends |
| Sermon | Tap **Sermon** | Sermon look; slides don't change the lights |
| Response song | Tap **Worship** (or tap a song section) | Tapping a section brings the worship lights back |

**When the band does something unplanned:**

- **Extra chorus:** tap **Chorus** on the Songs or Volunteer screen. In *Hybrid* mode the tracks take over again at their next section.
- **The tracks would jump somewhere wrong:** press **Hold** on the Songs tab. The tracks are ignored until you press it again.
- **You want to add energy:** hold **Flash** or **Strobe**, or tap a **pad** (Pulse, Chase, Sparkle…). See [Live control](live-effects.md).

**If it goes wrong:** hold **Panic** for 1 second. Everything goes back to the safe look at full brightness, in the Worship part.

## After the service

- Tap **Post-service** (if you have it), or a house-lights look.
- Changes you made during the service are saved automatically. If you changed something by mistake, **Undo** (↶ at the top) or restore a **snapshot** in Setup. See [Safety](safety.md).
- Turn the lights off at the end of the day, then the computer.

## Running it without a lighting person

The [Volunteer screen](volunteer-card.md) has only the big buttons: NEXT / BACK, the song's sections, the Service bar, Flash, Strobe, Blackout and Panic. Give volunteers a **volunteer PIN** (Setup → Access) so the rest of the app is locked.

With the tracks in **Auto** mode and ProPresenter following, a volunteer often only needs to tap the Service bar.
