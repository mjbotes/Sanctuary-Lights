# Volunteer quick card

*Print this page and keep it at the desk.*

## Before the service (5 minutes)

1. **Check the green dot** at the top right of the screen. Green means connected; grey means it isn't. Reload the page if it's grey.
2. Open the **Volunteer** tab. With a volunteer PIN, it opens by itself.
3. **Check that the lights respond.** Tap **Worship** in the Service bar; the stage should light up. If nothing happens, see *If something goes wrong* below.
4. **Pick the first song** from the *Pick a song…* list, unless the tracks or ProPresenter pick it for you.

## During the service

| You want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Go to the next part of the song | Tap **NEXT** (or press the space bar) |
| Go back | Tap **◀** (or the left arrow key) |
| Jump to a part (Chorus, Bridge…) | Tap its name |
| Switch to worship / hosting / sermon | Tap it in the **Service** bar at the top |
| Go to the next part of the service | Tap **Next item ▶** in the service order (if there is one) |
| Play a video | Nothing to do: the lights dim by themselves while it plays |
| Make everything flash | Hold **Flash** (or the **F** key) |
| Strobe | Hold **Strobe** (or the **S** key) |
| Turn everything off | **Blackout**. Tap it again to bring the lights back |

**The tracks or ProPresenter usually change the lights for you.** You only need to tap when something is missed, or when the band does something unplanned.

## If something goes wrong

- **Lights look wrong or are stuck:** **hold Panic for 1 second.** It stops every effect and strobe and goes back to the normal worship lighting.
- **Everything is dark:** check the **Blackout** button isn't on (it glows red when it is), and that the **Master** slider at the bottom is up.
- **Lights stay dim after a video:** tap **Worship** (or the part you're in) in the Service bar.
- **The screen says "Please enter your PIN":** type your volunteer PIN.
- **Still stuck?** Call the lighting team lead. The lights keep showing the last look, so there's no rush.

## Good to know

- Nothing you do on the Volunteer screen can break the show. The setup can't be changed from there.
- Lights that would shine on the projector screens may be switched off on purpose.
- You can run this screen from an iPad or phone on the church Wi-Fi as well.
