# Live control, pads and effects

Pre-programmed or busked: you can mix both. Everything here works **on top of** the cue list, songs and service parts, and **Release** puts things back.

## Hot buttons (every screen)

| Button / key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Strobe** / hold **S** | Strobes everything while held |
| **Flash** / hold **F** | Everything at full while held |
| **Blackout** / **B** | All off, on/off |
| **Panic** (hold 1 s) | Stops everything live and goes back to the safe look, at full brightness, in Worship |
| **Master** slider | Overall brightness |
| **T** | Tap the tempo |
| **R** | Release all live changes |
| **?** | Keyboard shortcuts (the **?** button opens this guide) |

## Live control per group

Every group (made on the Program tab) gets a strip on the Live and Songs tabs:

- **Fader:** trims that group's brightness (100% = as planned).
- **Colour buttons:** change its colour now, including **P / S / T** (the song's palette). **×** goes back to the planned colour.
- **Flash:** full while held.
- **Pulse / Chase / Sparkle / Rainbow / Move:** effects in time with the tempo. *Move* appears for moving heads; the speed menu sets beats per cycle. **More…** lists every effect.
- **Release live** clears all of it.

**The tempo** comes from **TAP** (or the **T** key), the song's BPM, or MIDI clock from Ableton. Click the BPM number to type one.

**Recording:** live colours and effects are saved into the next look or cue you record, so a *Chorus* look can always pulse the back wash on the beat. Fader trims and flashes are never recorded.

## Pads

Pads are your own live buttons on the Live and Songs tabs. Each one:

- runs an **effect**, applies a **preset** or **flashes**,
- on a group or all lights,
- as a **toggle** (tap on, tap off) or **hold** (only while pressed),
- with its own **key** (1–0 or any letter).

Ten come ready: Pulse, Chase, Sparkle, Wave, Rainbow, Beat strobe, Circle, Ballyhoo, Figure 8 and Breathe. **Edit pads** changes, adds, reorders or deletes them.

## FX & Presets tab

### Presets

Presets are reusable parts of a look: a **colour**, a **position**, a **dimmer** level or a **beam** setting.

1. Set the lights, select them, choose the type, and tap **+ Record preset**. Only that type is stored: a colour preset never changes brightness or position.
2. To apply one, select lights and tap the preset.
3. **They stay linked:** looks and cues remember which preset each light used. Update the preset (e.g. move the *Pulpit* position) and every look using it follows.

### Effects

About 25 effects are built in:

- **Dimmer:** pulse, chase, soft chase, wave, breathe, sparkle, odd/even, centre-out, strobes
- **Colour:** rainbow, colour cycle, two-colour chase, warm↔cool, fire flicker, palette chase / fade / spread
- **Moving heads:** circle, figure 8, ballyhoo, pan sweep, tilt saw, tilt wave, gentle move
- **Beam:** zoom pulse

Pick an effect, choose **selected lights / all / a group**, set the speed and press **▶ Run**. Running effects are listed with **speed** and **size** controls.

**Build your own** with **+ New effect**, or **Copy to edit** a built-in one. An effect has layers: what moves (intensity, colour, pan, tilt, zoom…), the wave shape, its range, and its speed. A **phase** spread decides how it travels across the lights:

- 0→0: all together
- 0→360: a smooth chase
- 0→180: odd/even

**Groups** and **Wings** make blocks and mirror images.

> **Selection order matters:** tap lights in the order a chase should travel, and they get numbered.

### Selection tricks

The **Selection tricks** panel changes the order and grouping of the selection without re-selecting:

- **Sort:** sort by position (left→right, centre→out…, or any 3D direction).
- **Shift / Shuffle:** rotate the order, or put it in a repeatable random order.
- **Block:** that many lights in a row share one step.
- **Groups:** the pattern repeats every N lights (Groups 2 = odd/even).
- **Wings:** mirror the pattern (Wings 2 = from the middle out).
- **Width / Active part:** work on part of the selection, stepping with ◀ ▶.
- **Fade / Delay From → To:** gives each light its own fade or delay in the next look you record, so changes ripple across the stage.

**Tricks on/off** pauses them; **Reset** clears them.

## Record live busking (live takes)

Busk a song once in rehearsal and it plays back the same way on Sunday:

1. Pick the song, and tap **● Record live take** (Songs tab, under NEXT).
2. Play the song with its tracks (Tracks tab), or with MIDI timecode if the song has a start timecode.
3. Busk: group faders, colours (including P / S / T), effects, Flash, Strobe, pads, live palette changes. **● REC** shows how many moves are recorded.
4. Tap **■ Stop & save**, or **Discard** to throw it away.

When the song plays, the **live take** plays your moves at the same moments in the music, on top of the section looks.

- It stays in time when you change the song's tempo.
- If the band repeats or skips a section (or you jump in the tracks), the take catches up to the right state for that point. Momentary flashes and strobes are left out.
- When the song stops, the take's live changes end too.
- Untick **Live take** to keep it but not play it; **×** deletes it.

You can still busk on top while a take plays.

## Busking a song

1. Start from the song's look (tap the section).
2. Bring a group up or down with its **fader**.
3. Tap **P / S / T** on a group to swap colours with the palette.
4. Tap **Pulse** on the back wash and **Move** on the movers in the chorus.
5. **Flash** on the big hits, **Strobe** for the drop.
6. **Release live** (or **R**) at the end of the song.
